ClinicDesk Roadmap

ClinicDesk is where patients book a visit without a phone call, and where the front desk sees the whole day in one calendar, built with a dental association and payment providers. It opens to every small clinic on 1 February 2027 with online booking. In the second half of 2027 it adds the health form before the visit. In 2028 it adds follow-up visits that patients book without a call.

The problem

Who we build for

A small clinic has one phone, and one person to answer it. That same person greets each patient at the counter. Patients call when they can, at lunch or after work, and often the line is busy. When a patient forgets a visit, the chair stays empty and the doctor waits.

We build for all of them. Here are five of their journeys, as examples. They are not everyone, but you may know someone in each one.

At the front desk

How do I answer the phone and look after the patient in front of me?

It is Monday, 9 in the morning, at a dental clinic with two doctors. The phone rings while one patient waits at the counter to pay and another asks where to sit.

The bookings live in a paper book, a chat app and the doctor's own phone. To find a free time, she checks all of them while the caller waits on the line.

A man comes to a physiotherapy clinic for his back. At the counter he gets three pages of paper and a pen.

Running the clinic

Why are chairs empty when the book was full?

The owner of a skin clinic counts four empty visits this week. Each one is an hour of her doctor's time with nobody in the chair.

Her front desk is meant to call each patient the day before. The calls take an hour, so on a busy day nobody makes them.

After the visit

Did she say two weeks, or four?

After his first session, a man with a sore knee is told to come back in two weeks. He means to call, and then work gets busy.

The clinic keeps a paper list of who should come back, and the front desk calls when it has time. A month later his knee hurts again, and he starts over.

Their questions differ, but most of them end in a phone call that should not be needed. Each of those calls costs time, at the desk and for the patient.

Maybe you saw yourself in one of them. Maybe you thought of the person at the desk of your own dentist.

Now imagine fewer calls and fewer empty chairs

A patient books in a minute, at any hour, and the front desk spends the morning on the people in the room.

The front desk opens the day and sees every booking in one calendar: from the booking page, from the counter and from the phone. The phone still rings, but less often, so she can look after the patient at the counter first.

The teacher with the sore tooth books at ten at night, from her bed. The man with the bad back fills in his health form at home, with his medicine boxes on the table, and the physiotherapist reads it before he walks in.

The owner sees fewer empty chairs, because each patient gets a reminder the day before. The man with the sore knee gets a link to book his next session, so nobody has to remember to call.

That is ClinicDesk. Part of it is already here today, and the plan below shows when the rest arrives.

Every visit, kept

At the end of a visit, the doctor sets when the patient should come back, and the patient gets a link to book it. A course of visits, such as six physiotherapy sessions, books in one go. Dental patients get a reminder for a check-up every six months.

Partners

By the end of 2028, a patient can go from the first booking to the follow-up without a phone call, and the front desk spends its day on the people in the room. Some patients will still prefer to call, and they can. We keep building after 2028, with the clinics that use ClinicDesk every day.

What it means for you

Whoever you are, there is a part of this plan for you. Find yourself below.

You are a patient

What you can use today, and what is comingMore
Today

If your clinic is one of the 12 that use ClinicDesk, open its booking page on your phone. Pick a doctor and a free time, and you get a message with your booking. You can still call the clinic if you prefer.

Your privacy

Your details belong to your clinic, not to us. We use them only to run your booking. We never sell them, and the booking page has no ads.

What is coming

In the second half of 2027, you fill in your health form at home, and you can move or cancel a visit yourself. In 2028, you book your follow-up from a link, with no call.

You own a clinic

What it costs, and what you get backMore
Why it matters

Each missed visit is an hour of a doctor's time with nobody in the chair. Each call to book takes your front desk away from the patient at the counter.

What exists today

A booking page and a shared calendar, used by 12 dental, skin and physiotherapy clinics with one to five doctors. The reminder the day before is half built. The health form and the follow-up link are not started.

What it takes

One monthly price for the clinic, however many doctors or bookings you have. To set it up, you enter your doctors, your opening hours and the kinds of visit you offer.

What would help most

We look for more clinics to try each new part before it opens to everyone, from March 2027. Write to hello@example.com.

You are a partner

What you get, and what we askMore
What you get

Payment providers: clinics that take deposits online from the second half of 2027. A dental association: a health form and check-up reminders that follow your advice, in the clinics of your members.

What we ask

Help us shape the forms and the rules for deposits. Tell your members when ClinicDesk opens to them. Write to hello@example.com.

What we promise

No partner pays for a better place on a booking page. Each clinic chooses its own payment provider. A payment provider sees patient details only when a patient pays a deposit through it.
