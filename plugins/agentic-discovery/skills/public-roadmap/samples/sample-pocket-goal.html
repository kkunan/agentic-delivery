Pocket Goal Roadmap

Pocket Goal is a savings app for people in their first jobs, built to work with the bank they already use. It opened in June 2026 to customers of our first partner bank, with goals and money you move with one tap. In 2027 it adds saving on payday by itself, at more banks. In 2028 it adds saving straight from your pay at work.

The problem

Who we build for

Most people in their first job want to save. Their pay lands, rent and bills go out, and a few weeks later the rest is gone. Nobody taught them how to save. Each month, saving is one more choice, on a day when something else comes first.

We build for all of them. Here are four of their journeys, as examples. They are not everyone, but you may know someone in each one.

First job, first pay

Where did my pay go?

He is 23, in his first office job. His pay lands on the 25th. By the 10th of the next month his account is close to zero, and he cannot say where the money went.

His bank offers a savings account, but he has to move the money himself, and he never does. A budget app asked him to log every coffee. He stopped after a week.

On paydayYour goal
Saving for one thing

How much do I put away to get there by March?

She is 26 and wants to visit her sister abroad next spring. She worked out the cost once, on the back of a receipt. She moves money when she remembers. By December she has a third of it.

Her savings account shows one balance. It does not know about the trip, so it cannot tell her that she is behind until it is too late.

Pay that changes

How can I save when I do not know what next month pays?

He works shifts at a hotel. One month he gets overtime, the next month he does not. He once set up a fixed transfer each month. In a slow month it left him short before rent, so he turned it off.

A fixed transfer does not know that his pay changes. To him, saving now feels like a risk.

First goal reached

I did it once. What now?

She saved for six months and bought the laptop she needed for her evening course. The goal sits at full, and she has not opened the app since.

Nothing asks her what comes next. Within two months, the money that went to her goal goes back to dinners out and taxis home.

Your goal

All of them want to save, and they know that they should. What stops them is the same: saving is a choice they have to make again every month, and the month always has something else to spend on.

Maybe you saw yourself in one of them. Maybe you thought of your son or daughter in their first job, or of someone new on your team.

Now imagine saving without thinking about it

Your pay lands, and a small part of it is already on its way to something you want, before you have time to spend it.

The man with his first pay sets one goal: one month of rent, put aside for a bad month. On payday, a small amount moves there by itself. He never logs a coffee. The woman saving for her trip sees a date and a line that tells her if she is on track. If she falls behind in November, she knows in November, not in March.

The man whose pay changes no longer fears a slow month. Before each move, Pocket Goal checks what he needs until his next payday. In a thin month it moves less, or nothing, and tells him why. The woman who bought her laptop is asked what comes next. The amount that went to the laptop now goes to her next goal, so the habit does not end with the first one.

None of them has to change bank. Their money stays in their own account, in their own name.

That is Pocket Goal. Part of it is already here today, and the plan below shows when the rest arrives.

Three moments we watch

How we know it works

Pocket Goal works when three things happen to the person who opens it. First, he sets a goal. Then he saves for it, payday after payday. When he reaches it, he comes back and sets the next one.

"My money has a plan, even when I do not."

The same plan, for you

What it means for you

Whoever you are, there is a part of this plan for you. Find yourself below.

You are in your first job

What you can use today, and what is comingMore
Today

If you bank with our first partner bank, get Pocket Goal and link your account. Set a goal with an amount and a date. On payday, move money to it with one tap, and watch the line grow. If you bank anywhere else, you cannot link your account yet.

Your privacy

Pocket Goal reads the balance and the payments in and out of the account you link. It uses them to spot payday, and from 2027, the bills due before the next one. We do not sell this, and nobody else sees it, not your employer and not your parents.

What is coming

In 2027, money moves to your goal on payday by itself, with a check so it never leaves you short, and two more banks join. In 2028, if your employer offers Pocket Goal, you can save straight from your pay.

How you can help

Tell us which bank you use, so we know which one to add next. Write to hello@example.com.

Your child is in their first job

How to help, without taking overMore
What it is

Pocket Goal is not a bank, and it never lends money. It moves money only between your child's own accounts, and it never holds the money itself.

What you see

Nothing, unless your child invites you. From 2027, they can share one goal with you. You see that goal, and you can add to it as a gift. You never see their balance or what they spend.

How to help

Send them the link, and talk about a first goal together. If they ask, tell them what you wish you had saved for at their age.

Questions

You hire people in their first job

What your staff get, and what we askMore
What you get

From 2028, you can offer Pocket Goal as a staff benefit. Your staff choose to send part of their pay to a goal on payday. You can add a small amount on top, if you wish.

What we ask

We look for a few employers to shape this with us during 2027, before it opens in 2028. Most of all, we need to learn how pay works at your firm. Write to hello@example.com.

What we promise

Each person decides whether to join and how much to save. They can stop at any time, without asking you. Pocket Goal never tells you who saves or how much.

You work at a bank

How Pocket Goal works with youMore
What you get

Your customers keep their money with you. Pocket Goal moves it between their own accounts at your bank, so their savings stay where they are. Young customers start a saving habit with the bank they already use.

What we ask

A safe way to link to your customers' accounts, with their consent, and to move money between their own accounts. We work with one bank today, and we plan two more in 2027. Write to hello@example.com.

What we promise

No bank pays to be shown first. We never ask your customers to move their money away from you. Each link needs the customer's yes, and they can remove it at any time.
