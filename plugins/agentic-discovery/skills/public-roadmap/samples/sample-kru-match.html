Kru Match Roadmap

Kru Match is where a high-school student and her parents find a tutor who fits her subject, her budget and her free hours, built with tutoring centers in Bangkok. It is open today to students in their last three years of high school, for maths and English, and in Q1 2027 families can book and pay in the app. In Q2 2027 it adds science, Thai and younger students. In Q3 2027 it adds exam help in small groups.

The problem

Who we build for

In Bangkok, most families find a tutor by asking around. A name comes from a LINE group or a friend, often with no price and no free hours. Tutors have the opposite problem. They have time to teach, but the families who need them do not know they exist.

We build for all of them. Here are five of their journeys, as examples. They are not everyone, but you may know someone in each one.

Behind in maths

Who can explain this the way my teacher does not?

She is in Mattayom 5, the fifth of six years of high school. Her maths test is in three weeks, and she lost the thread in week two. A tutoring center near Siam teaches her in a class of forty, and she is too shy to ask a question in front of them.

A friend gives her the name of a private tutor. He is booked until after the test, and nobody knows his price.

A parent who pays

Is this tutor any good, and what did my son learn today?

Her son has had an English tutor every Saturday for three months. She pays 500 baht in cash at the door, and gets no receipt.

She found the tutor through a parents' LINE group. She works late, so she never sees a lesson. She cannot tell if his English is better, or if she pays for the same lesson again and again.

A tutor at university

How do I find students who fit my free hours?

He is in his third year of engineering, and he teaches maths to pay his rent. Last week a parent cancelled an hour before the lesson, after he had crossed the city on the BTS.

He posts in Facebook groups, and few parents reply. A tutoring center offered him students, but it keeps half of what parents pay, and it pays him at the end of the month, sometimes late.

A new subject

My English is fine now. Who can help me with physics?

He had a good English tutor all last term. The new school year starts in May, and physics is the subject he fears.

His English tutor does not teach physics. He starts the search from zero in the same LINE groups, and his notes from last term sit in a paper folder at home.

A teacher after school

Can I teach two evenings a week near home, and be paid on time?

She teaches chemistry at a public school, and her evenings are free. Parents at her own school ask her to tutor their children, but she does not want to take money from her own students.

Families near her home do not know that she teaches. The few who find her pay late, or forget.

Five people, five different questions. Under each one is the same gap: the student, the parent and the tutor cannot see each other clearly. So they guess, and a wrong guess can cost a whole term.

Maybe you saw yourself in one of them. Maybe you thought of a child before an exam, or a friend who tutors to pay for university.

Now imagine the right tutor in one evening

One place where a student, her parents and a tutor find each other, and keep every lesson in view.

The girl who is behind in maths searches on Sunday evening. She asks for Mattayom 5 maths, under 400 baht an hour, on Tuesday after school. Three tutors fit, and she books one for that week. The boy who fears physics finds a physics tutor in the same place in May, and his English notes from last term are still there.

The mother who paid cash at the door now sees the price before she pays. After each lesson, the tutor writes what they covered and what to practice. She reads it on the bus home, and she no longer has to guess what her money buys.

The engineering student gets bookings that fit his free hours, near his campus or online, and he is paid within three days. The chemistry teacher fills two evenings a week with students from other schools, close to her home.

That is Kru Match. Part of it is already here today, and the plan below shows when the rest arrives.

Q1 2027 to Q3 2027

Quarter by quarter, subject by subject

We plan by quarters, because families plan by school term. Kru Match opened on 1 August 2026 for maths and English, for students in Mattayom 4 to 6. Q1 2027 finishes that part before the term 2 finals. Q2 and Q3 2027 open it to more subjects and more students.

Each part below shows its period, who it helps most, and what it adds to each of the three promises.

The same plan, for you

What it means for you

Whoever you are, there is a part of this plan for you. Find yourself below.

You are a student or a parent

What you can use today, and what is comingMore
Today

If you are in Mattayom 4 to 6 in Bangkok and need help with maths or English, open Kru Match. Choose the subject and the price you can pay, and see the tutors who fit. Each tutor writes their free hours in their profile, but you cannot search by them yet. To book, you chat with our team on LINE, and you pay by bank transfer before the lesson.

Your privacy

A tutor sees the student's first name, school year and subject. The full name, the school and the parent's phone number stay hidden until the parent books. From Q1 2027, lesson notes are seen only by the student, the parent and that tutor.

What is coming

In Q1 2027, you book a time and pay by QR code in the app, and you read the tutor's notes after each lesson. In Q2 2027, physics, chemistry, biology and Thai arrive, and so do students from Mattayom 1. In Q3 2027, you can learn for the entrance exams in a small group of friends, so each of you pays less.

How you can help

Tell us which subject you searched for and did not find. Write to hello@example.com.

You are a tutor

How families find you, and how you are paidMore
Today

You can join if you teach maths or English to students in Mattayom 4 to 6. We talk with you by video and check your ID card before your first booking. Families find you by subject and by price.

How you are paid

You keep 85 baht of every 100 that a family pays. Today we pay you by bank transfer at the end of each month. From Q1 2027, we pay you within three days of each lesson.

What is coming

In Q1 2027, you set your free hours and get only bookings that fit them. In Q2 2027, you can teach science or Thai, and younger students. In Q3 2027, you can teach small groups, and see your bookings and earnings for the whole term.

How you can help

If you teach physics, chemistry, biology or Thai, join us before May 2027, so students find you when the school year starts. Write to hello@example.com.

You run a school or a tutoring center

What you get, and what we askMore
What you get

A school gets a place to send parents who ask for a tutor, so teachers do not have to name one. A tutoring center gets bookings for its empty rooms, and for its teachers' free hours.

What we ask

Share Kru Match with parents at the start of each term. Tell us the subjects your students struggle with most, so we find tutors for them first. Write to hello@example.com.

What we promise

No school or center pays for a better place in search. A student's details never go to a partner without the parent's yes. We start with two centers, and we hope to welcome the first schools before May 2027.

You want to support Kru Match

Why it matters, and what it takesMore
Why it matters

Bangkok has many good tutors, but families find them through who they know. A family without the right friends pays more, or pays for a big class where the child cannot ask a question.

What exists today

Since 1 August 2026, about 40 tutors teach maths and English to the first families. Our team does the booking and the payments by hand. Lesson notes, reviews and search by free hours are not built yet.

What it takes

Two founders full time and one developer part time. Savings and the fee on each lesson pay for it today, and the fee does not yet cover the team.

What would help most

Science and Thai tutors before May 2027, and money to build payments in the app in Q1 2027. Write to hello@example.com.
